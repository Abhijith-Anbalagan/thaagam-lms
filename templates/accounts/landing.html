One platform for every role in your school.

EduPlatform is a unified school management system built to connect every role in an educational institution — from super admins overseeing multiple schools, to school admins managing their campus, to management teams, teachers, and students. We replaced the mess of disconnected tools with one intelligent platform where everyone sees exactly what they need. Admins get full control. Teachers get clarity. Students get focus. Built with role-based access, real-time data, and seamless communication at its core, EduPlatform is not just software — it is the operating system for modern schools. One platform. Every role. Zero confusion.

School Administration

Super admins manage schools end-to-end — onboard school admins, assign courses, track performance, and get platform-wide analytics at a glance.

Management & Teachers

Management teams oversee classrooms and teachers. Teachers run their classes, post assignments, and track every student's progress — all in one place.

Student Learning

Students join classrooms with a simple code, access course content, submit assignments, and see their own progress — with no confusion about where to go.

Our Aim

Replace the chaos of multiple tools
with one clear, role-based system.

Most schools use one app for attendance, another for assignments, another for communication — and nothing talks to each other. EduPlatform replaces all of that with a single platform where every user sees exactly what they need, nothing more.

Our goal is simple — give every student a guided, connected learning experience by making sure the people around them are always in sync.
